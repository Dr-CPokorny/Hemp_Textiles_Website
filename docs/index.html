Help Shape the Future of Hemp in the Textile Arts

Learn about hemp fabrics, explore material testing results, and share your perspective as we considerhow hemp may fit within quiltmaking and the textile arts.

"Make It With Hemp" brings together makers, educators, and researchers in a community-based exploration of hemp fabrics in quiltmaking and the textile arts.
Whether you are an experienced quiltmaker, a shop owner, or simply curious about hemp fabrics, your perspective helps us better understand how these materials are perceived and used.
